Northstar Health AI Challenge

Hosted by Northstar Health Foundation.

Key Dates

Submissions close January 15, 2027. Finalists are announced February 1, 2027 and present on March 4, 2027.

Eligibility

Open to clinicians, operators and builders aged 18 or older. Teams of up to four people.

Finalists may present remotely or in person at the Northstar Innovation Center in Boston. Travel is optional.

Submit a working prototype, a 3-minute demo video and a one-page impact summary.
